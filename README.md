# mygithubpage

## Making session

`making-session/index.html` is a single-page tool for short making sessions with clay, drawing, sculpture, or design.

A session has these steps:

1. Check-in: rate how settled you feel and your energy.
2. Put your distractions away and do nothing for one minute.
3. Write down what's on your mind for five minutes. Save one task for after.
4. List what you're curious about.
5. Choose a material and something small to try.
6. Make for 20 to 90 minutes with your phone away.
7. Second check-in, tap what you noticed, and write what you learned about yourself.

Your notes show past sessions plus statistics and trends. Nothing is scored, and there are no streaks.

## Schedule and check-ins

The tool has two tabs. "Make" is for sessions. "Schedule" is for planning.

- **Plan sessions:** pick days, a time, and a length for one week (this week or next week), then commit to the plan. Plans do not repeat. Each week gets its own plan.
- **Add to calendar:** after you commit, each session has a link that adds one event to Google Calendar.
- **Weekly check-in:** compares planned and finished sessions for the week, records what got in the way and what to change, and leads into planning next week. Sunday morning works well.

The plan for this week and next week is saved to the artifact database (`shared/plan`) so a Claude check-in can read it.
