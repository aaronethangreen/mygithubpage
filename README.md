## Making session

`making-session/index.html` is a single-page tool for short making sessions with clay, drawing, sculpture, or design.

A session has these steps:

1. Check-in: rate how settled you feel and your energy.
2. Put your distractions away and do nothing for one minute.
3. Write down what's on your mind for five minutes. Save one task for after.
4. List what you're curious about.
5. Choose a material and something small to try.
6. Make for 20 to 90 minutes with your phone away.
7. Second check-in, tap what you noticed, and write what you learned about yourself.

Your notes show past sessions plus statistics and trends. Nothing is scored, and there are no streaks or reminders.

Sessions are saved in the browser. The notes screen has a button to download them as a JSON file.

Writing rules for this repo are in `CLAUDE.md`.
